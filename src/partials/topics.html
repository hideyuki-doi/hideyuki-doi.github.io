Large-scale data analysis for biology

Integrating species records, DNA, satellite images, long-term observations and published studies to find general rules across fields: species richness estimation, biological communities, agriculture, and life from the deep sea to cities.

Biological community

How communities of fish, plankton, fungi and microbes are assembled and how they change across space and time, through field surveys, metabarcoding of environmental DNA, large-scale distribution data such as GBIF, and DNA preserved in sediments.

{{PART:icon-climate}}

Climate change biology

How the timing of flowering, leaf colouring and insect emergence responds to climate change across Japan, read from decades of phenological records.

Food webs

What sets food-chain length, and how food webs are structured in space and time in lakes, rivers, tidal flats and coasts, using stable isotopes.
