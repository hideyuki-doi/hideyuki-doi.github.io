Hideyuki Doi
Biosphere Informatics Lab., Graduate School of Informatics, Kyoto University

We study ecology at its boundary with informatics, looking for general rules in nature, from bacteria to large mammals and from ponds to the deep sea, with field data, environmental DNA, long-term records and large-scale analysis.

生態学と情報学の境界で、生物学や生態学における一般性の検証を目指しています。
